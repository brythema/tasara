What is a Merchant Steward

A verified participant in the TASARA network.

A Merchant Steward is a seller or business that has been verified and registered within the TASARA network. Stewards are the backbone of the system — the people and businesses whose trade activity creates the track record that the network is built on.

Every Steward is verified before going live. This means buyers can trade with confidence, knowing the person on the other end has been authenticated within the network.

Stewardship is organized into four tiers, each reflecting the scale and scope of the participant's trade activity.

Verification

Every Steward is verified before going live.

Verification protects the entire network. When you apply as a Merchant Steward, an Administrator reviews your application to confirm your identity and business legitimacy. This ensures that every participant in the TASARA network can trade with confidence.

The verification process typically takes 24–48 hours during active pilot periods. You'll receive a notification once your application has been decided.
