Common questions

Everything you need to know.

Click "Create your account" and select the Seller role. You'll walk through a three-step process: your details, tier selection, and document upload. Every application is reviewed by an Administrator before approval.

Signing up as a System Participant (buyer) is free. Becoming a Merchant Steward involves a Liquidity Stake tied to your chosen tier — it funds your initial Omni issuance and registers you as a vested participant in the network.

TASARA is currently in active pilot in Otukpo, Nigeria. Expansion to Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt is planned. The system is designed for continental replication — the same structure, taken across West Africa and beyond.

We're always looking for partners who share our vision. Email us at tasaraafrica@gmail.com with details about your organization and how you see collaboration working. We review all partnership proposals personally.
