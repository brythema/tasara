The transaction

From chat to confirmed record.

The process begins exactly where real trade already happens — in a WhatsApp conversation between a buyer and a seller. No new app to learn. No complicated setup. Just a structured record of what was agreed, who agreed to it, and what was delivered.

The seller shares product details, the buyer confirms the order, and both parties' participation gets recorded in the TASARA network. This isn't a theoretical ledger — it's a persistent, verifiable track record that travels with them.

Over time, consistent good work builds economic standing. That standing opens doors — to better terms, more customers, access to credit, and inclusion in a growing continental network.

The record

"A trade with no record is a trade that disappears. A trade inside TASARA never does."

Why this matters

Without a record, your work disappears.

In the informal economy — where most African trade happens — there is no credit history, no verified track record, no persistent identity beyond what you can carry in your pocket.

TASARA fills that gap. Every verified trade becomes part of a growing record. Every consistent participant builds economic standing that can be recognized by lenders, partners, and institutions that previously had no way to assess them.

This isn't about replacing how people already trade. It's about making sure that trade counts — that it leaves something behind.
