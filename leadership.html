Founding

The vision behind TASARA.

TASARA is an initiative and trademark of Iron Mint Stewardship Limited (RC 9380856), founded by Emmanuel Adakole Michael.

The idea emerged from observing a simple but profound gap: the vast majority of economic activity across Africa happens outside formal records. People trade, build, serve, and create value every day — but none of it counts in any lasting way.

TASARA was built to change that. Not by imposing foreign systems, but by giving structure to what already exists — the WhatsApp conversations, the market transactions, the verbal agreements that form the backbone of African commerce.
